Questions, answered

Before we light anything.

Is the photo estimate really free?

Yes. Send clear photos and your address, and we will prepare an initial estimate at no cost. A site check may be needed before final confirmation on complex properties.

Do I need to measure my roofline?

No. Photos and the property address are normally enough for an initial estimate. We handle measurement and material calculations.

Can you install lights I already own?

Yes, provided the lights and wiring are appropriate, complete, and in safe working condition. We will review them before installation.

What does full service include?

Design, commercial-grade or project-appropriate lighting materials, installation, reasonable maintenance during the season, and removal. Optional storage may be quoted separately.

How do I reserve a date?

After you approve the estimate, a 50% deposit reserves the installation date. Remaining payment terms will be shown clearly on the final quote.

Where do you work?

Illuminated is based in Waco, Texas, and serves Waco and surrounding Central Texas communities. Travel availability depends on the project.
